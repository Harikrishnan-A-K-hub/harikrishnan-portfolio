About

Hey. I'm Harikrishnan A Kurup.

I'm 17 and I build things for the web. Sites, apps and whatever else I can talk a browser into doing. Design and code, usually at the same time.

In July 2026 I founded MW Studios, a web design studio that builds websites and web apps. Right now it is a one person operation. I handle the design, the build and the deploy. That is deliberate. I would rather ship a handful of things I am proud of than spread myself thin. The team can grow when the work does.

Web design is the craft I love. Cybersecurity is where I am headed. The long term goal is to work in security at a company doing serious work. Think the Google, Anthropic, NVIDIA and SpaceX tier. MW Studios is how I keep my hands dirty while I get there.
